2008 Chevrolet Malibu LS

A long-term LE5 2.4L development platform used for learning calibration, improving drivability, testing intake and exhaust changes, and planning a much more serious future version of the car.

Work includes HP Tuners scanning and calibration changes, MAF calibration development, fuel-trim analysis, deceleration behavior testing, exhaust development, and ongoing drivetrain planning.
