Before Scheduling

Know the current setupBring a complete list of modifications, fuel type, current tune, and anything recently changed.
Describe symptoms clearlyWhen does the issue happen? RPM, speed, gear, load, temperature, and warning lights all help.
Mechanical condition mattersA tune should not be used to hide mechanical faults. Problems need to be diagnosed before calibration work is treated as the fix.
Data is usefulExisting scan logs, DTCs, wideband data, and previous calibration files can shorten the diagnostic process.
Requests are priority reviewedSubmitting a request does not guarantee acceptance or a specific date. Higher-priority work and projects that are a better fit for the current schedule may be handled first.
